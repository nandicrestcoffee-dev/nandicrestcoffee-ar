## ☕ Overview

**Nandi Crest Coffee** focuses on single-origin high-altitude Kenyan Arabica sourced from **Nandi Hills** and coordinated through our strategic trade desk in **Nairobi** for international roasters, importers, and commercial coffee partners[cite: 1].

### Commercial & Logistics Highlights
* **Trade Terms:** **FOB** (Mombasa) and **CIF** (Destination Port)
* **Shipping Modes:** **Sea Freight** (20ft / 40ft FCL & LCL containers) and **Air Freight** (priority shipments & coffee samples)
* **Export Documentation:** Commercial Invoice, Certificate of Origin, Packing List, and Quality / Moisture Specifications
